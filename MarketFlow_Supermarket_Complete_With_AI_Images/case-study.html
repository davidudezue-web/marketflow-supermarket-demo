04 / RECOMMENDATIONS

Translate analysis
into testable actions.

REVENUE

Protect high-demand categories

Track department contribution, margin and stock availability. Test cross-category promotions before scaling.

KPIs: revenue, gross margin, stockouts
DEMAND

Plan for customer peaks

Compare store-specific traffic, experiment with staffing and monitor queue times and conversion.

KPIs: transactions/hour, wait time
INVENTORY

Control perishables waste

Audit waste definitions, reconcile stock movements and pilot smaller replenishment batches.

KPIs: weighted waste, sell-through
